OUR MISSION

Together, We Can Make A Difference

Plastic pollution is one of the biggest environmental challenges facing our planet. PlasticLess helps people understand the problem and turn awareness into action.

Our platform brings together campaigns, educational resources and simple daily tasks that anyone can complete to reduce plastic consumption.
